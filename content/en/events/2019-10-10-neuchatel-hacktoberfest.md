The 2019 Hacktoberfest gathering invited participants to contribute to Jenkins
and related open-source projects, with experienced Jenkins contributors
available to advise them.

Suggested activities included adopting Jenkins Pipeline or Configuration as Code
in open-source projects, improving Configuration as Code compatibility in
plugins, and documenting missing Jenkins features or migrating documentation
from the wiki. Participants could also suggest their own topics.

### Scheduled program

- **09:30:** Doors open.
- **10:00:** Oleg Nenashev introduces Hacktoberfest and Jenkins.
- **10:15:** Oleg demonstrates how to contribute to Jenkins.
- **11:00:** Team work and knowledge sharing.
- **18:00:** Wrap-up and demonstrations.
