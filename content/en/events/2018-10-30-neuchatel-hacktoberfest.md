This Hacktoberfest gathering invited participants to contribute to Jenkins and
other open-source projects. Experienced Jenkins contributors were available to
help, whether participants wanted to work on Jenkins itself or add a Jenkinsfile
to another open-source project.

The proposed knowledge-sharing sessions included creating Jenkins X build packs
with Cosmin Cojocar and contributing to jenkins.io with Oleg Nenashev.
Additional sessions could be arranged according to participants' interests.

### Scheduled program

- **09:30:** Doors open.
- **10:00:** Oleg Nenashev introduces Hacktoberfest and Jenkins development.
- **11:00:** Team work and knowledge sharing.
- **18:00:** Wrap-up and demonstrations.

See the Jenkins project's [2018 Hacktoberfest announcement](https://www.jenkins.io/blog/2018/10/01/hacktoberfest/)
for more background.
